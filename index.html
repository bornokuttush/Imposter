IMPOSTER

How to Play

Most players receive the same secret subject. The Imposter does not know it.

Everyone gives a clue without making the subject too obvious.

After everyone gives a clue, discuss the answers and secretly vote.

If the Imposter is eliminated, they get one chance to guess the secret subject.

Catch all the Imposters before they gain control.
