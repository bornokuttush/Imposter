RULES

How to play

Pass the phone around. Everyone secretly receives a role. Crew members know the subject. The Imposter doesn't.

1. Reveal
Each player privately checks their role.

2. Clues
Every living player gives one clue without saying the secret subject.

3. Discussion
Compare everyone's clues and decide who seems suspicious.

4. Secret vote
Pass the phone again. Everyone votes privately. Nobody can vote for themselves.

5. Final guess
If an Imposter is eliminated, they get one chance to guess the subject.

6. Win conditions
Crew wins when all Imposters are eliminated. Imposters win if they reach parity with Crew or correctly guess the subject.
